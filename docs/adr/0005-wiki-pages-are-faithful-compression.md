# Wiki Pages are a faithful compression of their Sources

Every LLM step of Ingest (extraction, condensation, page writing, summaries) compresses what the cited Sources say: each sentence restates something the cited text states or directly implies, in the Source's own framing and terms. The model adds no knowledge of its own (no dates, names, context, or explanations that the text does not give), no evaluation (no praise, criticism, opinions, or judgments of truth), and keeps attribution when the Source presents something as a claim or belief. We chose fidelity over helpfulness: a page can be less complete than what the model knows, but a user and their agents can trust that every sentence traces back to a cited passage. One shared rule block in the prompts holds this policy, and a faithfulness evaluation (claims checked against their resolved passages) measures it.
